Every bug, glitch and gripe called out in the "Did They Even Test This?" segment of the Waveform podcast, and whether the company has fixed it yet. Tap a number to filter.

Fan-made and not affiliated with Waveform, MKBHD or Vox Media. Items were gathered from episode transcripts, which can mishear names, so who raised a gripe is sometimes marked unknown. "Fixed" is only shown when there is a public source link. "Can't verify" means only the person with the gadget can check, like a host's own car.

Spotted a fix or a missing gripe? Send the episode and a link and it goes into the next audit.
